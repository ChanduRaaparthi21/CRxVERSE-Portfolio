About Chandu Raparthi

Crafting Immersive
Interior Realities

I'm Chandu Raparthi, a 3D interior designer based in Hyderabad with over 30 completed projects. I specialize in transforming architectural concepts into stunning photorealistic visualizations that help clients see, feel, and approve their spaces before construction begins.

From residential living rooms to commercial spaces, every render I create is built with meticulous attention to lighting, texture, and spatial composition — because the details make all the difference.
